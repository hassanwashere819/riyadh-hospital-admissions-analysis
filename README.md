# riyadh-hospital-admissions-analysis
Exploratory and statistical analysis of hospital admissions in Riyadh from 2020 to 2024, examining monthly trends, differences across hospitals, seasonality, and whether length of stay and emergency visits predict admission volume.
This project analyzes hospital admission data across multiple hospitals in Riyadh over a five-year period. The focus is on understanding admission patterns over time, comparing hospitals against each other statistically, and testing whether operational features can predict admission volume.

Results: The ANOVA test found no statistically significant difference in admission counts across hospitals (F-statistic 0.735, p-value 0.642). The seasonal decomposition and rolling average show the underlying trend and seasonal pattern in admissions over the five years. The linear regression result is essentially null (R² of about -0.0001, RMSE of 1.43), meaning length of stay and emergency visit count alone don't explain admission volume. This should be presented as a genuine finding, not a failure: it tells you those two features aren't the right predictors and points toward exploring other variables such as condition type, severity level, or seasonal indicators instead.
